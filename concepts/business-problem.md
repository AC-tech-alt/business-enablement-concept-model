# Business Problem

## Definition

A gap between current and desired state, named by a domain leader, that is significant enough to warrant a coordinated response. Not yet a plan of action — just a validated need.

## Properties

- **Owning domain** — which business area identified the problem
- **Urgency** — how time-sensitive resolution is
- **Scope** — single-site, multi-site, or enterprise-wide
- **Current-state evidence** — the data or observation that substantiates the gap (not just an assumption)

## Relationships

- A Business Problem may generate one [Initiative](./initiative.md), several, or none.
- A Business Problem sets the target that [Value Realization](./value-realization.md) is later measured against.

## Rules

- A Business Problem can exist without an Initiative — it can sit unaddressed, be deprioritized, or wait for resourcing. The reverse is not true.
- A Business Problem should be re-validated if a significant amount of time passes before an Initiative is chartered against it — the underlying evidence may no longer hold.
