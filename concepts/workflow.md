# Workflow

## Definition

The specific, designed sequence of steps, roles, and system touchpoints that operationalizes part of an [Initiative](./initiative.md). A Workflow is a design artifact — it describes how work *should* move, not evidence that it moves that way yet.

## Properties

- **Process owner** — who is accountable for the design
- **Systems touched** — which platforms or tools the workflow runs through
- **Upstream/downstream dependencies** — what has to happen before and after this workflow for it to function

## Relationships

- One or more Workflows belong to a single [Initiative](./initiative.md).
- A Workflow has one [Execution Plan](./execution-plan.md).

## Rules

- A Workflow being "designed" and a Workflow being "used" are different states. Design completion should never be reported as if it were [Adoption](./adoption.md).
- A Workflow can be revised after Field feedback without the underlying Initiative or Business Problem changing.
