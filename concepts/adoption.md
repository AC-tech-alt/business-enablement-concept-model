# Adoption

## Definition

Sustained, observed use of a [Workflow](./workflow.md) as designed, under normal operating pressure — not a one-time training attendance or a go-live date.

## Properties

- **Usage rate over time** — not a single snapshot
- **Variance from designed process** — how much the real-world practice has drifted from the Workflow as designed
- **Duration of sustained use** — how long the usage rate has held, since Adoption can regress

## Relationships

- Adoption follows [Field Readiness](./field-readiness.md).
- Adoption is the precondition that must hold before [Value Realization](./value-realization.md) can be meaningfully evaluated.

## Rules

- Adoption is measured after go-live, on a lag, and can regress — it is a status that has to be re-checked, not a milestone you hit once and keep.
- An Initiative marked "launched" is not the same as one marked "adopted." These should never share a status field.
