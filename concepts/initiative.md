# Initiative

## Definition

The formally chartered effort to close a [Business Problem](./business-problem.md). An Initiative can span multiple Workflows and multiple owners over its life.

## Properties

- **Sponsor** — the accountable business leader
- **Target outcome** — what success looks like, ideally quantified
- **Timeline** — chartered start and target completion
- **Status** — see rule below on what status values actually mean

## Relationships

- An Initiative is chartered against exactly one [Business Problem](./business-problem.md) (though a Business Problem may later spawn additional Initiatives).
- An Initiative is broken into one or more [Workflows](./workflow.md).
- An Initiative's success is ultimately evaluated through [Value Realization](./value-realization.md).

## Rules

- An Initiative cannot exist without a documented Business Problem.
- "Status" on an Initiative should never collapse Execution, Field Readiness, and Adoption into a single value like "Complete." Each of those is tracked separately — see [relationships.md](../relationships.md) for why conflating them is the most common failure mode in status reporting.
