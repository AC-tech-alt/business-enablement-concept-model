# Worked Example: Grant Compliance Reporting Workflow

A second fictional case, this time from grantmaking rather than healthcare, to show the model isn't specific to one domain — the same chain applies wherever a strategic priority has to move through design, execution, and sustained use.

## The chain

**[Business Problem](../concepts/business-problem.md)**
Named by the Grants Director: 28% of subrecipients submit compliance reports late or incomplete, creating installment payment delays and audit exposure at year-end.
*Owning domain:* Grants/Compliance. *Urgency:* High (audit cycle approaching). *Scope:* Portfolio-wide. *Evidence:* Reporting timeliness data pulled from the grants management system over two fiscal years.

**[Initiative](../concepts/initiative.md)** — "Compliance Reporting Reliability"
Sponsor: Director of Grants. Target outcome: reduce late/incomplete reports to under 10%. Timeline: 120 days, ahead of the next audit cycle.

**[Workflow](../concepts/workflow.md)**
Designed by Grants Operations: automated reminders tied to each installment's reporting due date, a standardized report template, and a compliance checklist subrecipients complete before submission is accepted. Process owner: Grants Operations. Systems touched: grants management platform. Dependency: relies on accurate installment schedules already being entered in the system.

**[Execution Plan](../concepts/execution-plan.md)**
Owned by Grants Operations and Systems jointly. Sequencing: (1) build automated reminder logic in the platform, (2) pilot with 10 subrecipients, (3) train program officers on the new checklist, (4) roll out portfolio-wide.

**[Field Readiness](../concepts/field-readiness.md)**
Program officers trained on the new checklist and how to flag incomplete submissions. Subrecipients notified of the new reporting portal and given a walkthrough. One issue surfaced and fixed during the pilot: reminder emails were landing in spam for some subrecipients — sender authentication was corrected before full rollout.

**[Adoption](../concepts/adoption.md)**
Tracked monthly starting at rollout: % of reports submitted on time and complete, through the portal rather than by other means.
- Month 1: 61% (early, most subrecipients still adjusting)
- - Month 2: 74%
  - - Month 3: 79%, holding
   
    - A subset of subrecipients — mostly smaller, under-resourced organizations — continue emailing reports directly to program officers instead of using the portal, bypassing the checklist entirely. Flagged as an adoption gap tied to capacity, not willingness.
   
    - **[Value Realization](../concepts/value-realization.md)**
    - Evaluated after Adoption held for 90+ days. Late/incomplete rate dropped from 28% to 15% — short of the under-10% target, with the gap concentrated almost entirely among the subrecipients still bypassing the portal.
   
    - ## Why this matters: two ways to report the same initiative
   
    - **Naive status report:**
   
    - > Compliance Reporting Reliability: ✅ Complete
      >
      > **Model-based status report:**
      >
      > | Concept | Status |
      > |---|---|
      > | Workflow designed | Complete |
      > | Execution Plan | Complete |
      > | Field Readiness | Complete, portfolio-wide |
      > | Adoption | Partial — 79% sustained, gap concentrated among smaller subrecipients |
      > | Value Realization | 15% vs. under-10% target — gap traceable to adoption, not workflow design |
      >
      > The naive version tells the board the initiative shipped on time. The model-based version tells them exactly which segment of subrecipients still needs a different kind of support — likely a lighter-weight submission path, not more training — which is the actionable insight a collapsed status field would have hidden.
